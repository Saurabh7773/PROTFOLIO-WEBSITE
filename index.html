\ABOUT ME\\
\

I am 20 years old, currently in 3rd year (Graduating 2028), pursuing a career in Computer Science & Engineering in specialisation of Ai & Ds from Poornima University, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. Born and brought up in Jodhpur, Rajasthan. I have done my schooling from Army Public School Jodhpur, Rajasthan in 2024, with Science + Computers as my background. \

\

My idea behind taking up Computer Science & Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is my love for programming that has being made in my class 12 while working with Python Programming Language and the fact that, given the knowledge, I can make a computer do almost anything I want it to do. \

\

I have a keen interest in the field of Machine Learning and Data Science, and I'm presently learning and working on the skills required to expertise in the same. \

\

My hobbies include reading blogs, novels on current and advanced technologies evolving, such as Ai related researches reading articles, browsing YouTube recommendations, traveling, and binge watching series. \

\

I am a firm believer of passion and determination.\
